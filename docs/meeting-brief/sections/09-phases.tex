\section{Delivery Phases}\label{sec:phases}

\begin{plainwords}
We build in five phases. Nothing big-bang: each phase ends with something demonstrable.
The system becomes safe to pilot after Phase 2, complete enough to go live after Phase 3,
and connected to DPD and Xero after Phase 4. Week ranges are planning estimates from the
assessment document and assume the team sizes assumed there.
\end{plainwords}

\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{3.1cm} X p{2.1cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Phase} & \textbf{Deliverables} & \textbf{Estimate} \\
\midrule
\textbf{0 -- Discovery \& domain freeze} &
  Formal rule register from the v11 assessment, entity-relationship model, state-machine
  diagrams, permission matrix confirmed with the client, data-quality report from the
  backup, migration mapping, DPD/Xero scope agreement & 2--3 weeks \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{1 -- Secure foundation} &
  Central authentication with roles, user management, database, customer master, job
  creation, product grid with size allocation, search/sort, audit skeleton, file
  storage, backups, deployment and monitoring & 4--6 weeks \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{2 -- Operational core} &
  Department stages with Active/Upcoming/Blocked/Completed views, stock receipts and
  outstanding quantities, screens readiness, print positions, traffic-light readiness,
  queue sorting, role-restricted stage updates, stage notes/waste/reprints, role-filtered
  Excel export, concurrency handling & 6--8 weeks \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{3 -- Approvals \& control (go-live MVP)} &
  Artwork approval with versions, swatch workflow with production blocking, dispatch and
  shipment records with part dispatch and void, central file storage, audit viewer,
  staged Excel import, export tooling, migration executed, training & 4--6 weeks \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{4 -- Integrations \& optimisation} &
  DPD API adapter (booking, labels, reprint, manual fallback), print
  request/confirmation workflow, Xero adapter with outbox pattern, dashboards,
  notifications, performance work & 4--8 weeks \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\begin{center}
\footnotesize
\textit{Cumulative rough timeline: limited pilot possible after $\approx$11--17 weeks;
full go-live after $\approx$16--23 weeks; integrations complete by
$\approx$20--31 weeks.}
\end{center}
