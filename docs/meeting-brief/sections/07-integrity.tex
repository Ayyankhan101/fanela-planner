\section{How Data Is Protected}\label{sec:integrity}

\begin{plainwords}
In a factory, history matters: who approved what, when a shipment left, how much stock
arrived. The new system therefore never truly deletes important records -- it cancels,
voids or corrects them with a reason, and writes it down. Two logs (stock history and
operational audit) are append-only, even against a developer making a mistake.
\end{plainwords}

\subsection*{Integrity rules}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}X X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Rule} & \textbf{How it is enforced} \\
\midrule
No hard delete of jobs, shipments, swatch attempts, approvals, stock or audit records &
  Application has no delete path; database role denies DELETE \\
Stock history and audit are append-only & Insert/select-only database role; significant
  changes store before/after snapshots as JSON \\
Approved swatch attempts are immutable & Application rejects edits \emph{and} a
  database row-level security policy (\texttt{FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY}, policy
  \texttt{status <> 'Approved'}) makes approved rows unwritable at the engine level --
  even if code bypasses the application layer. A blanket \texttt{REVOKE UPDATE} is not
  used: it would also block legitimate status transitions \\
Revising approved artwork resets it to Draft & State machine + audit entry in the same
  transaction \\
Embroidery blocked while a required swatch is unapproved & Stage service re-checks the
  gate on every transition \\
A job completes only when all stages and dispatch are complete & Computed; cannot be set
  directly \\
Void is not delete & The shipment keeps its booking rows; the void is an event with a
  reason \\
Concurrent edits are safe & Version numbers \emph{scoped to the sub-entity being
  edited}: stages, shipments, swatch attempts and order lines have their own
  \texttt{version} columns, so a stock receipt never conflicts with an unrelated
  dispatch-date edit; mismatch returns 409 plus the current record \\
Multi-step actions are atomic & Stage completion, swatch decisions, stock receipts,
  booking/final dispatch and import confirmation all commit as one transaction \\
Blank requirements block readiness & A blank ``screens required'' field means
  \emph{pending specification}, never zero -- the gate stays closed \\
Files are private & Access only through the owning record's permission check, via
  short-lived signed links; approved swatch files are write-once; labels survive voids \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection*{Sessions and login}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Email + password, hashed with Argon2id; multi-factor (TOTP) mandatory for Admin,
        Operations and Dispatch; recovery codes shown once.
  \item Server-side session cookie: http-only, secure, same-site; \textbf{2-hour idle
        timeout} (an unattended factory terminal re-logs-in well before shift end) and
        \textbf{12-hour absolute cap} (fresh login at the end of a long shift regardless
        of activity); logout destroys the session row. Idle must be strictly shorter
        than the absolute cap -- a 12 h / 12 h pairing would make the idle rule dead
        code.
  \item Login attempts are rate-limited per email and per address, with progressive
        lockout; failures are logged.
  \item Open question for the meeting: confirm the 2-hour idle value and whether shared
        terminals also want a quick PIN re-lock screen
        (Section~\ref{sec:decisions}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Backups}

Nightly database dump plus a copy of the file store, both transferred off the server,
retained at least 30 days (period to confirm), with a documented restore procedure. The
prototype's JSON backup remains available as an export format.
