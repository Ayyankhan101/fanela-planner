\section{Who Can Do What}\label{sec:permissions}

\begin{plainwords}
Seven roles: Admin, Operations Manager, Office, Director, Dispatch, Packing and
Department Operator. The interface hides buttons the user cannot use, but the \emph{server}
is the judge -- every API call is checked independently, including which columns of data
the reply may contain (for example, costs never leave the server for Office users).
\end{plainwords}

\subsection*{Roles at a glance}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Role} & \textbf{Scope} \\
\midrule
Admin & Everything: users, roles, backups, all jobs and data \\
Operations Manager & All jobs; approves artwork and swatches; edits dispatch; views
  costs; audit \\
Office & Creates/edits jobs, customer master, planned dispatch, Excel. \textbf{No}
  buying costs \\
Director & Read-only oversight; costs in reports only; no editing \\
Dispatch & Dispatch details, bookings, final dispatch, void. \textbf{No} costs \\
Packing & Read-only dispatch information and relevant stages \\
Department Operator & Updates \textbf{own department's stage only}; no costs; no
  approvals \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection*{Permission matrix}

Key: \textbf{Y} yes \quad \textbf{Y*} only with an extra grant \quad \textbf{R}
read-only \quad \textbf{L} limited scope \quad \textbf{N} no

\textbf{Y*} means an additional grant exists (``authorised commercial'' flag for prices,
department assignment for stages/swatches).

\subsection*{How it is enforced}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The matrix lives in the database (\texttt{role\_permissions}) and the same
        catalogue drives both the interface and the API checks -- they cannot drift apart.
  \item Every mutating endpoint is covered by automated ``permission probe'' tests: call
        it as each role, expect exactly the right allow/deny.
  \item Costs and prices are stripped in the serialiser before the response is built,
        including in Excel exports (the worker decides columns at write time: Director's
        copy includes costs, Office's copy does not).
\end{itemize}
